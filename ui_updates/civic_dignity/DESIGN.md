## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for civic infrastructure, blue-collar empowerment, and sovereign verification workflows. The visual identity rejects fleeting digital trends in favor of institutional permanence, absolute clarity, and unyielding dignity for every worker and citizen navigating the platform.

### Core Tenets
- **Institutional Reliability:** The UI communicates the permanence and security of an essential public utility, establishing immediate trust between workers, employers, and public institutions.
- **Cognitive Equity:** Interfaces are stripped of superfluous decoration to lower cognitive load. Visual hierarchy is direct, transparent, and forgiving of low-bandwidth connectivity, varying digital literacy, and multi-script multilingual reading environments.
- **Physical Tangibility:** Digital credentials, escrow transactions, and work logs feel substantial and definitive, anchoring verification in grounded, high-contrast visual signifiers.

### Design Style
The system employs a **Dignified Modern Civic** style—combining the clean structural logic of Swiss institutional grids with warm, grounded micro-interactions. The interface utilizes structured cards, deliberate surface delineation, high contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for standard copy and 7:1 for vital statuses), and multi-sensory dual signifiers (shape + color + icon + text) that ensure zero reliance on color alone.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for outdoors readability, varying mobile display qualities, and unambiguous status tracking.

### Primary Palette
- **Deep Teal (`#0D5C5B`):** The primary brand anchor. Used for primary interactive triggers, structural headers, high-level navigation bars, and active state indicators.
- **Warm Amber / Golden Marigold (`#D97706`):** The secondary highlight tone. Designated for pending milestones, critical attention notices, escrow holds, and active verifications in progress.
- **Trust Green (`#15803D`):** The tertiary validation tone. Reserved strictly for settled escrows, authenticated biometric badges, verified civic identity, and confirmed completed contracts.
- **Civic Slate Neutral (`#1E293B`):** Deep charcoal-slate providing uncompromised readability for dense instructional typography across varying display backlights.

### Surface and Structural Tones
- **Base Canvas (`#F4F6F6`):** A soft, eye-resting off-white providing sustained comfort during prolonged administrative or field sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white reserved for operational cards, credential sheets, and interactive modal dialogs.
- **Structural Border (`#E2E8F0`):** Subtle structural divider ensuring crisp definition between layered surfaces without visual friction.
- **Muted Supporting Text (`#64748B`):** Neutral intermediate shade tuned to pass AA compliance on white card surfaces.

### Contrast Discipline
Every interactive or semantic state must maintain dual-layer accessibility. Color is never deployed as an isolated indicator; status colors must always wrap high-contrast icons and textual labels.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all textual hierarchies. Its wide counterforms, generous x-height, and open apertures preserve high legibility across low-resolution mobile devices while providing seamless vertical metrics when rendering Devanagari scripts alongside Latin typefaces.

### Multilingual Script Management & Text Expansion
- **Devanagari Vertical Rhythm:** Hindi and Marathi feature continuous headline strokes (shirorekha) and extensive matras above and below the baseline. All line heights maintain an expansive ratio (minimum 1.4x to 1.6x) to completely eliminate ascender/descender clipping.
- **Horizontal Expansion Budget:** UI components (action bars, tabs, floating trigger buttons) must allocate a minimum of 30% additional horizontal container padding to accommodate text expansion during localization transitions from English to Hindi or Marathi.
- **Fixed Widths Prohibited:** Buttons and status chips must scale dynamically with inline content; explicit static widths are strictly disallowed.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout operates strictly on an **8px base unit rhythm** (0.5rem), providing predictable touch boundaries and fluid adaptation across edge-to-edge mobile screens and wide desktop supervisor consoles.

### Grid Framework
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column layout with fluid 4-column sub-divisions. Fixed outer margin of `margin` (16px / 1rem) and internal column gutter of `gutter` (16px / 1rem). Stacked vertical cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins and gutters. Side-by-side verification panels and dual action streams.
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column layout with `margin-desktop` (32px / 2rem) and `gutter-desktop` (24px / 1.5rem), maxing out at an ergonomic reading boundary of 1280px centered container width.

### Ergonomic Spacing Rules
- **Interactive Target Padding:** Any component receiving touch input must yield a bounding box of at least 48px × 48px, padded by `space-sm` or `space-md`.
- **Card Interior Density:** Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports to prioritize dense situational awareness without visual tension.

## Elevation & Depth

To guarantee reliability in harsh outdoor sunlight and low-contrast display environments, visual depth prioritizes crisp surface demarcation and subtle ambient shadows over heavy, diffuse blurs.

### Surface Layering Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Ground):** `#F4F6F6` baseline tone.
2. **Structural Surfaces (Cards & Containers):** `#FFFFFF` crisp panels framed by a 1px solid stroke of `#E2E8F0`. Depth is communicated primarily through this border, not deep shadowing.
3. **Floating & Navigational Layers (Bottom Sheets, App Bars):** Elevated via a crisp ambient shadow tinted with primary teal undertones: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 92, 91, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
4. **Modal Overlays & Critical Verification Sheets:** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a 60% opacity deep slate backdrop filter (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`).

### Outline & Edge Fidelity
Every card, chip, and input control features a visible bounding edge. Flat floating elements without borders are prohibited to prevent blending under high ambient light conditions.

## Shapes

The shape system utilizes deliberate, approachable rounding that balances official institutional authority with human warmth. 

### Geometric Rationale
- **Base Rounding (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Inputs, secondary buttons, standard notification tiles, and micro-controls.
- **Card Rounding (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Operational cards, credential passes, identity panels, and elevated sheets.
- **Pill Rounding (`rounded-full`):** Reserved solely for Dual-Signifier Status Badges and verified biometric markers, highlighting their status as self-contained tokens of truth.

## Components

### 1. Dual-Signifier Status Badges
Status chips must convey state through three concurrent mechanisms: color token, dedicated SVG icon, and unambiguous textual label.
- **Verified Escrow / Identity:** Deep Green background tint (`#DCFCE7`), solid green border (`#86EFAC`), bold Trust Green text (`#15803D`), leading checkmark shield icon (`size: 16px`).
- **Pending / Action Required:** Soft Amber background tint (`#FEF3C7`), amber border (`#FCD34D`), bold Golden Amber text (`#B45309`), leading clock/alert indicator icon (`size: 16px`).
- **Neutral / Draft:** Soft Slate background (`#F1F5F9`), slate border (`#CBD5E1`), text `#475569`, leading document/pencil icon.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Civic Action:** Solid Deep Teal background (`#0D5C5B`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded` (8px), height 48px minimum. Hover state darkens to `#0B4F50`. Focused state displays a 2px offset ring in `#0D5C5B`.
- **Secondary Outlined Action:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in `#0D5C5B`, text in `#0D5C5B`. Internal padding: `12px 20px`.
- **Destructive Action:** Crimson tint background (`#FEF2F2`), border (`#FECACA`), text `#B91C1C`.

### 3. Cards & Credential Blocks
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-xl` (24px), framed by a 1px solid stroke of `#E2E8F0`.
- Card headers separate administrative category from verification status using dual-signifier badges aligned opposite to section headlines.
- Critical data points (e.g., daily wage, escrow balance, hours logged) utilize bold display numbering with small, distinct baseline labels.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Bounding height of 48px with internal horizontal padding of 16px (`space-md`).
- Surface color `#FFFFFF` with 1.5px solid border `#CBD5E1`. On focus, border transitions to `#0D5C5B` with an ambient glow of `rgba(13, 92, 91, 0.15)`.
- Labels sit permanently outside and above the field in `label-md` (`#1E293B`) to avoid disappearing placeholder disorientation. Helper text resides underneath in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### 5. Checkboxes, Radios, and Selection Tiles
- Selection targets feature a minimum tap surface of 48px × 48px.
- Checkbox bounding box: 20px × 20px, 4px border radius. Unselected uses 1.5px border `#94A3B8`. Selected uses `#0D5C5B` fill with solid white check mark.
- Selection cards: Used for multi-lingual language pickers or role definitions. Enclosed in `rounded-lg` borders that transition from `#E2E8F0` to a 2px `#0D5C5B` border with `#F0FDFA` background on active selection.

### 6. Escrow & Contract Lock Banners
- Dedicated institutional components featuring a high-contrast left border (4px solid `#15803D` for locked, `#D97706` for processing).
- Includes secure lock iconography, explicit monetary breakdown figures, and cryptographic / civic ID hash stamps rendered in compact, readable labels.
