## Brand & Style

The design language reflects institutional integrity, human dignity, and mutual empowerment. Built for skilled tradespeople, domestic workers, independent contractors, and urban consumers across India, the interface balances enterprise-grade dependability with extreme functional clarity. It directly counters the alienation and precarity of gig-economy platforms by visually emphasizing cooperative ownership, fair compensation, and credentialed verification.

The visual style is **Corporate / Modern** anchored in high-contrast utility and tactile reassurance:
- **Clarity over cleverness:** Zero ornamental glassmorphism, glowing gradients, or decorative micro-interactions that degrade performance on low-spec hardware or confuse first-time digital users.
- **Physical reassurance:** High-contrast structural boundaries (1px crisp borders), predictable touch anchors (minimum 48px target bounds), and warm institutional tones that project the stability of a cooperative bank or civic utility.
- **Cultural resonance:** Dignified presentation of skilled blue-collar and pink-collar professions, using honest photographic framing and clear verification iconography rather than gamified rating gimmicks.

## Colors

The palette delivers immediate institutional trust paired with warmth, engineered specifically for high outdoor visibility and varying mobile screen quality.

- **Primary (`#1A2B4C` - Deep Cooperative Navy):** Communicates authority, stability, and enterprise structure. Used for primary interactive actions, persistent navigation bars, prominent headings, and active state indicators.
- **Secondary (`#E65A28` - Terracotta Saffron):** An empowering, energetic civic accent drawn from terracotta craft traditions. Reserved for call-to-action buttons, high-priority operational badges, urgent confirmations, and actionable alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#1E7E34` - Verified Forest Green):** Represents cooperative validation, escrow milestone payouts, verified skill credentials, and system health.
- **Surface & Background Architecture:**
  - App Canvas: `#F8F9FA` (Warm Off-White) to reduce glare in bright sunlight.
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
  - Outlines / Dividers: `#E5E7EB` (Subtle 1px Neutral Border).
- **Typography & Semantic Colors:**
  - Text Primary: `#191C1E` (Dark Charcoal) for uncompromising WCAG AAA readability.
  - Text Secondary: `#596066` (Subdued Slate) for metadata, captions, and secondary cues.
  - Warning: `#D97706` (Warm Amber) for pending documents, OTP timers, and dispute notices.
  - Error: `#DC2626` (Crimson Red) for payment failures, validation rejections, and critical alerts.

## Typography

The dual-type strategy pairs the approachable, geometric authority of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and status titles with the systematic, high-legibility rendering of **Inter** for dense transactional tables, booking instructions, and numeric inputs.

- **Indian Rupee Formatting:** Financial amounts must always use the standard Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) formatted with standard Indian grouping (e.g., `₹1,24,500`), set in medium or bold weight to avoid optical clipping on standard low-DPI displays.
- **Multilingual Readiness:** Font choices must support clear baseline matching when switching between English and Indic scripts (such as Devanagari, Tamil, or Bengali). Line heights are set intentionally generous (minimum 1.4x for body copy) to avoid ascender/descender collisions when localized.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Display and headline levels are reserved for key screens, account status summaries, and section anchors. Labels maintain heavier weights (`600`) to guarantee legibility at small sizes during outdoor operation.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout system operates on an 8px base rhythm (with a 4px half-step for micro-alignments like badges and icons). The layout model follows a **fluid-within-fixed container** strategy, prioritizing handheld single-thumb operation while cleanly scaling to tablets and service center desktop kiosks.

- **Grid Systems by Form Factor:**
  - **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid grid. Outside margins are fixed to `1rem` (16px) with `1rem` gutters. All primary action bars are pinned to the bottom viewport with a persistent 48px to 56px height.
  - **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid. Outside margins scale to `2rem` (32px), enabling split-pane navigation (e.g., service list on the left, booking details on the right).
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column fixed grid with a max container width of `1200px` centered on the viewport. Gutters remain `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Physical Touch Target Rule:** All tap targets (buttons, list item chevron anchors, checkbox surfaces, segment toggles) must strictly resolve to a minimum bounding box of **48px × 48px** (`3rem`), even if visual elements appear smaller.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid system of **1px structural strokes and low-diffusion ambient shadows**. This maintains tactile separation in harsh ambient light without relying on heavy blur filters that degrade rendering performance on budget Android devices.

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Background canvas set at `#F8F9FA`. Used for screen backdrops and unselected group containers. Flat, 0px offset.
- **Card & List Tier (Level 1):** White `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle 1px stroke (`#E5E7EB`) and a soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.04)`. This gives immediate lift to verified worker profiles and job order cards.
- **Raised Interactive Elements (Level 2):** Dropdowns, active filters, and floating status banners use: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.04)` with a `#E5E7EB` border.
- **Overlays, Bottom Sheets & Modals (Level 3):** Fixed bottom sheets (standard booking workflow drawers) use: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.06)`, topped with a persistent 16% opacity scrim (`#191C1E` at 0.54 alpha) to maintain clear contrast with underlying content.

## Shapes

The design uses a rounded geometry level of **2** (`0.5rem` / 8px default, scaling to `0.75rem` / 12px for standard cards and inputs, and `1rem` / 16px for larger container surfaces). 

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips:** Standardized at `12px` corner radius. This balances human warmth with precise functional discipline, avoiding excessive bubble styling while eliminating harsh rectangular corners.
- **Feature Cards and Bottom Sheets:** Scaled to `16px` radius on top or outer corners to group complex information (such as cooperative identity cards and job estimates).
- **Verification Badges & Avatars:** Cooperative member badges use a `6px` radius for small markers, while worker profile portraits use circular masks (`9999px`) or `16px` rounded squares with an integrated 2px green cooperative-verified ring.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** Height `48px` to `52px`. Solid `#1A2B4C` (Deep Navy) background, pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), `12px` corner radius. Minimum touch target `48px`. Hover/Pressed state: `#132038`.
- **Secondary / Urgency Button:** Height `48px`. Solid `#E65A28` (Terracotta Saffron), white text. Used for time-sensitive booking, confirming payouts, or emergency support requests.
- **Secondary Outline Button:** Height `48px`. Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in `#1A2B4C`, text in `#1A2B4C`.
- **Disabled State:** Solid `#E5E7EB` fill, `#9CA3AF` text, non-interactive cursor, no elevation.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Height `52px` with integrated internal padding (`16px`). Clean `1px` border stroke (`#D1D5DB`), background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus state triggers a `2px` crisp border in `#1A2B4C` with zero glowing halos.
- **Rupee Amount Inputs:** Leading `₹` glyph hard-coded at `18px` bold (`#596066`), followed by right-aligned or high-contrast tabular figures.
- **Helper & Validation Labels:** Positioned directly below the input in `13px` Inter (`#DC2626` for error, `#596066` for neutral helper text). Never rely on color alone; errors always prepend a standard warning icon.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Cooperative Worker Profile Card:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, `12px` radius, 1px `#E5E7EB` border. Includes worker portrait, verified check badge (`#1E7E34`), cooperative federation unit name, transparent trade experience tag, and clear pricing rate (e.g., `₹450 / hr`).
- **Escrow / Payment Card:** Distinct container with a soft `#F8F9FA` inner fill, crisp `1px` border, displaying total locked cooperative deposit, released milestones, and an instant bank transfer guarantee tag.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Standard Filter Chip:** Minimum height `40px` (with `48px` target bounds), padding `8px 16px`. Default state has `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E5E7EB` stroke, `#191C1E` text. Selected state has `#1A2B4C` background, white text, and a crisp checkmark prefix.

### Status Indicators & Timelines
- **Booking / Service Progress Tracker:** Linear vertical or horizontal pipeline using 24px circular nodes connected by 2px solid lines.
  - Completed: Solid `#1E7E34` circle with white checkmark.
  - In-Progress: Solid `#1A2B4C` circle with an active pulsing inner dot.
  - Pending: White circle with a 2px `#E5E7EB` border.
- **Badges:** Pill-style with `4px` horizontal padding and `2px` vertical padding.
  - *Verified Cooperative Worker:* Subtle `#E8F5E9` green background with `#1E7E34` text and an authentic shield check icon.
  - *Dispute / Review:* Subtle `#FEF3C7` amber background with `#D97706` text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Size:** `24px × 24px` box or circle with minimum `48px` touch bounds.
- **Active State:** Solid `#1A2B4C` with high-contrast white glyph.
- **Border:** 2px solid `#596066` in inactive state, preventing wash-out on low-contrast mobile screens.
